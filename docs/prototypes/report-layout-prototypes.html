Decisions in this prototype

Each card is one choice. The tag shows my recommendation, and the buttons set the workspace above so you can try it. Scroll back up to see it.

Panels you can move recommended

Every panel (Dictation, Sections, Templates) has a grip for dragging and a dock button for moving it with one click: left, right, bottom, floating, or hidden. The layout is remembered between sessions.

  • Drop zones only appear while you drag, so the normal screen stays clean.
  • Every drag has a click alternative, so it works without dragging.

Mic on the right, stacked vertically default

The report gets the full height. The mic, timer, level, dictation target and session timeline are stacked in one column. "Classic" keeps today's bottom bar for people who prefer it.

Section navigator recommended

A numbered list of the report's sections, each with a one-line summary. Click to jump, or press 1–6. The section you're reading is highlighted, and the one you're dictating into has a red ring.

Pin any section recommended

Hover a section heading and click the pin. Pinned sections stay in a tray under the report, so the Impression is always in view while you scroll through long Findings. Impression is pinned by default. The tray shows 3 lines and opens to full on click.

Folding with summaries

Collapsed sections show a one-line summary, so the report reads like an index. "Collapse all" leaves pinned sections and the one you're dictating into open.

Visible dictation point recommended

You always see where words will land. It shows in the report (caret and label) and in the Dictation panel ("Findings › Lungs"), and in the top bar of the report. Click any line to move it, even while recording; what you've said so far stays where it was. Phrases insert at the same spot.

Reading controls

Text size (+/−) only changes the report, not the chrome. Line width is capped at a comfortable length (--ws-measure). Indent guides show the hierarchy and can be turned off.

Focus mode optional

F hides every panel and leaves the report with a small floating mic in the corner. Templates are still one key away (T). Good on laptops.

Templates on demand

The book icon in the top bar (T) shows or hides the Templates & phrases panel wherever it is docked. People who never use templates hide it once and never see it again.
